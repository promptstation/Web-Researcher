# Course 11 — Natural Language Processing (NLP) & LLMs
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 11: Natural Language Processing (NLP) & LLMs
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Text Cleaning and Normalization**

**Summary:** How to clean scraped text with encoding repair, whitespace and boilerplate removal, dedupe, and language-aware normalization that preserves meaning.

**Absorbed Skill:** Clean scraped text with repaired encoding, removed boilerplate, and preserved meaning.

---

**Language Detection and Multilingual Ops**

**Summary:** How to detect languages reliably, route per-language pipelines, handle code-switching and scripts, and evaluate multilingual quality fairly.

**Absorbed Skill:** Detect languages reliably and route per-language pipelines with fair eval.

---

**Chunking and Embeddings Prep**

**Summary:** How sentence-aware chunking with overlap, metadata carryover, token budgeting, and embedding hygiene prepare corpora for retrieval that actually retrieves.

**Absorbed Skill:** Chunk corpora for retrieval with sentence awareness, metadata, and budgets.

---

**LLM Extraction to Structure**

**Summary:** How constrained LLM extraction with schemas, validation, retry, and field-level eval turns messy text into trustworthy structured records.

**Absorbed Skill:** Extract structured records with LLMs under schema, validation, and field eval.

---

**Data-task Prompt Engineering**

**Summary:** How versioned prompts with instructions, examples, constraints, and eval harnesses reliably classify, normalize, dedupe, and route scraped data.

**Absorbed Skill:** Engineer versioned data-task prompts with examples, constraints, and eval gates.

---

**RAG Pipelines with Citations**

**Summary:** How retrieve-rerank-generate pipelines with grounded citations, abstention, freshness filters, and faithfulness eval answer from your corpus honestly.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build grounded RAG with citations, abstention, and faithfulness eval.

---

**LLM Eval and Guardrails**

**Summary:** How red-teaming, PII scrubbing, toxicity and bias checks, prompt-injection defenses, and output validators keep LLM pipelines safe in production.

**Absorbed Skill:** Ship LLM features with red-teaming, PII scrubbing, and layered guardrails.

---

**Fine-tuning and Distillation Decisions**

**Summary:** How to decide between prompting, RAG, fine-tuning, and distillation with pilots and evals, and how to run small fine-tunes with clean data and honest measurement.

**Absorbed Skill:** Choose adaptation methods by pilot eval and run clean small fine-tunes.

---

**LLM Cost and Latency Operations**

**Summary:** How token budgets, caching, batching, model routing, and latency SLOs keep LLM pipelines affordable and fast without quality loss.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run LLM pipelines inside token budgets and latency SLOs with routing and caching.

---

**Entity Resolution and KB Building**

**Summary:** How mention normalization, blocking, pairwise matching, clustering, and survivorship build knowledge bases from scraped text with provenance.

**Absorbed Skill:** Resolve entities and build knowledge bases with provenance and survivorship.
