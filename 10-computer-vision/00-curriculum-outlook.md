# Course 10 — Computer Vision (CV)
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 10: Computer Vision (CV)
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Image Acquisition and Dataset Building**

**Summary:** How to collect images lawfully with licenses, dedupe exactly and perceptually, record provenance, and build versioned datasets teams can trust.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build lawful, deduped, versioned image datasets with provenance.

---

**Preprocessing and Augmentation**

**Summary:** How resize, crop, normalize, color, and augmentation pipelines prepare images for models, with deterministic eval transforms and inspected training transforms.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build inspected preprocessing and augmentation pipelines with deterministic eval paths.

---

**Image Classification Operations**

**Summary:** How to run transfer-learning classification: stratified splits, fine-tuning schedules, class imbalance handling, calibration, and error analysis that ships.

**Absorbed Skill:** Ship image classifiers with stratified splits, tuned training, and error analysis.

---

**Object Detection Operations**

**Summary:** How to annotate boxes consistently, validate COCO datasets, train detectors, read mAP honestly, and handle small, dense, and overlapping objects.

**Absorbed Skill:** Ship detectors with consistent boxes, validated data, and honest mAP.

---

**OCR Operations**

**Summary:** How to preprocess document images, choose OCR engines, evaluate with CER/WER, handle tables and handwriting honestly, and route low-confidence output to review.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run OCR with measured CER/WER, honest engine choice, and review routing.

---

**Visual Privacy and Redaction**

**Summary:** How to detect faces, plates, and screens, redact with blur or blackout, verify redaction coverage, and govern visual PII with consent and retention.

**Absorbed Skill:** Govern visual PII with detected, verified redaction and consent discipline.

---

**Video and Frame Operations**

**Summary:** How to sample frames by need, extract with ffmpeg discipline, dedupe near-identical frames, track objects across time, and budget video storage honestly.

**Absorbed Skill:** Sample, extract, and dedupe video frames with budgeted storage and tracking.

---

**Visual Web Extraction**

**Summary:** How screenshots, visual locators, layout analysis, and golden-image regression complement DOM extraction when markup fails or layout is the signal.

**Absorbed Skill:** Complement DOM extraction with screenshots, visual locators, and layout analysis.

---

**Model Evaluation and Error Analysis**

**Summary:** How to evaluate vision models beyond headline metrics with slices, calibration, robustness probes, fairness checks, and error galleries that drive data fixes.

**Absorbed Skill:** Evaluate vision models with slices, calibration, robustness, and fairness — then fix data.

---

**Vision Deployment and Monitoring**

**Summary:** How to package vision models with cards, serve with latency budgets, canary releases, monitor drift and slice metrics live, and roll back safely.

**Absorbed Skill:** Deploy vision models with cards, canaries, live slice monitoring, and safe rollback.
