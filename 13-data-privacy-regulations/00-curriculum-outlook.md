# Course 13 — Data Privacy Regulations
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 13: Data Privacy Regulations
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Privacy Landscape Literacy**

**Summary:** How GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL, LGPD, and sectoral laws compare on scope, rights, bases, transfers, and penalties, and how to map which laws govern each data flow.

**Absorbed Skill:** Map data flows to applicable privacy laws with compared duties and rights.

---

**Lawful Basis and Purpose Limitation**

**Summary:** How to select and document lawful bases, define narrow purposes, run necessity and proportionality checks, and block purpose creep in pipelines.

**Absorbed Skill:** Ground every collection in documented basis, purpose, and necessity checks.

---

**Consent Management**

**Summary:** How to design lawful consent (freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous), record it tamper-evidently, honor withdrawal instantly, and audit consent chains.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run consent that is freely given, recorded, withdrawable, and auditable.

---

**Data Minimization in Pipelines**

**Summary:** How field-level necessity reviews, collection guards, aggregation, and deletion defaults keep pipelines minimal by design and default.

**Absorbed Skill:** Keep pipelines minimal with field reviews, guards, and deletion defaults.

---

**DSAR Operations**

**Summary:** How intake, identity verification, search across systems, redaction of third parties, and on-time response make rights requests routine and provable.

**Absorbed Skill:** Handle rights requests on time with verified identity and complete search.

---

**Retention and Deletion Proof**

**Summary:** How retention schedules, automated deletion, backup alignment, and verification proofs satisfy storage limitation with evidence.

**Absorbed Skill:** Enforce retention with automated deletion and verification proofs.

---

**Cross-border Transfers**

**Summary:** How adequacy, SCCs, BCRs, certifications, and transfer impact assessments govern moving personal data across borders lawfully.

**Absorbed Skill:** Govern cross-border transfers with registered mechanisms and impact assessments.

---

**DPIAs and Risk Assessment**

**Summary:** How to screen for high risk, run DPIAs with necessity, risk, and mitigation analysis, consult DPO and subjects, and track mitigations to done.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run DPIAs that find real risks and track mitigations to done.

---

**Vendors and Processors**

**Summary:** How vendor due diligence, DPAs with flow-downs, subprocessor control, audits, and exit plans keep processors inside your Records perimeter.

**Absorbed Skill:** Keep processors compliant with diligence, DPAs, audits, and exits.

---

**Breach Response**

**Summary:** How detection, containment, 72-hour notification assessment, subject communication, and postmortems turn breaches into controlled, disclosed, learned-from incidents.

**Absorbed Skill:** Respond to breaches with fast containment, lawful notification, and learning.
