# Processor Control

Tiering: high (sensitive/volume/critical: full diligence + yearly audit), medium (standard DPA + biennial), low (terms review). Diligence: security posture (certs, pen tests), privacy (DPA stance, subs, incidents history), financial/continuity. Evidence filed, not vibes.

DPAs must: process only on instructions; confidentiality; security measures; subprocessor flow-down + change notice/objection; assist rights/DPIAs; breach notice without undue delay (support your 72h); audit rights; delete-or-return at end with proof; transfer mechanisms for routes. Legal reviews every DPA; signed before any personal data flows.

Subprocessors: listed with purpose/location; 30-day change notice; objection right with exit; re-diligence highs. Audits: yearly highs (evidence: certs, logs, walkthroughs); remediation owned/dated; renewals gated on closure. Vendor breach = your clock: contain, scope, notify, postmortem.

Exits: export in usable form; delete incl. backups/subs with proof; verify absence; close file. Shadow SaaS discovery via SSO/spend sweeps; retro-process or sunset.
