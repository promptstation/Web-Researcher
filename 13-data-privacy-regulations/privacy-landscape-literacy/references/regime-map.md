# Regime Map

GDPR (EU/EEA): broad personal-data scope; 6 lawful bases; rights incl. erasure/portability/objection; DPIAs for high-risk; 72h breach notice; transfers need adequacy/SCCs+safeguards; fines to 4 percent turnover. Establishment + targeting tests.

CCPA/CPRA (California): consumer scope with thresholds; opt-out (sale/share), limit-use, deletion/correction; no DPIA mandate but risk assessments for high-risk; 45-day response; private right + agency fines. Watch sister US state laws (similar cores, varied details).

PIPL (China): consent-forward (separate consent for sensitive/cross-border); security assessments for volume/critical transfers; local storage expectations; rights incl. explanation; strict cross-border. LGPD (Brazil): GDPR-like bases/rights with ANPD guidance evolving.

Ops rule: scope = subjects location + your establishment + targeting behavior; highest-common-denominator controls where flows overlap; counsel confirms gray; literacy enables questions, counsel gives answers. Track EDPB/ICO/CAC/AG guidance monthly.
