# Deletion Proof

Schedules: per store x purpose (hot/warm/delete ages + legal basis); legal sign-off; holds dated with owners, monthly review, auto-expire nudges. Delete jobs: dry-run -> approve -> run -> verify -> log; bulk deletes two-person approved.

Backups: purge-cycle alignment (deleted in prod disappears by next cycle) or crypto-shredding (per-subject keys destroyed); restore checklists re-apply deletes; vendors contractually aligned with evidence. Test restores verify deletes hold.

Verification: targeted search for subject/keys across prod+backup-index+logs (zero hits); bulk purges sample-verified (n=100+); deletion logs immutable. Proof pack: scope, method, timestamps, verification output, actor — filed per purge and per DSAR.

Reporting: quarterly retention compliance (stores on-schedule, holds open, deletes run, verifications passed); exceptions with owners and dates. Storage limitation is continuous, not a project.
