# DPIA Practice

Screen triggers (any 2+ = DPIA likely): sensitive/special data, large scale, systematic evaluation/profiling, public monitoring, new tech, vulnerable subjects, cross-border risk, automated decisions with effects. Gate project intake; log screen-outs with reasons.

DPIA: describe processing (flows, data, retention, vendors); necessity vs purpose; risks scored (likelihood x severity to rights: discrimination, loss of control, chilling, breach harm); mitigations per risk (minimize, secure, transparency, choice, contract); residual rating; DPO opinion; subject consultation where apt; authority consultation if residual high.

Mitigations: owner + date each; launch-gated on musts; verified done (not just planned); monitored live (metrics per mitigation). Residual high risk unmitigable = escalate: redesign, narrow, or authority-consult before processing.

Living: register of DPIAs with review dates; review on flow/vendor/law change or yearly; versioned; regulator-ready export. DPIA quality judged by mitigations shipped, not pages written.
