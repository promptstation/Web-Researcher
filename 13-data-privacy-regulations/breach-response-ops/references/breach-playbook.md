# Breach Playbook

Clock: awareness starts it; authority notification without undue delay, 72h where GDPR applies (reasons if late); subjects without undue delay when high risk; document timeline obsessively. DPO + counsel looped in hour one.

Contain: revoke keys, isolate systems, close exposure, preserve evidence (freeze logs, image where needed); scope (categories, volume, subjects, sensitivity, exfil proof); assume-worst where logs gap, say so honestly.

Notify content (authority): nature, categories + approx volumes, DPO contact, likely consequences, measures taken/proposed. Subjects: plain language, what happened, what data, what you do, what they can do, support offered (monitoring where apt). Phased notification allowed as facts develop.

Register: every breach (even non-notifiable) with facts, effects, remediation — authority can demand it. Postmortem 2 weeks: root cause, controls, training, drill update. Tabletop yearly minimum; angry-timeline review after each real one. Cover-ups end careers and companies.
