# Consent Lifecycle

Valid consent: freely given (no detriment for refusal), specific (per purpose), informed (who, why, rights, withdrawal), unambiguous (affirmative action). Bans: pre-ticked boxes, bundled T&Cs consent, cookie walls for essentials-alternatives, nagging, unequal prominence, Roach-motel withdrawal.

Evidence per grant: subject id, purposes, notice version, timestamp, method/UX snapshot, IP/device coarse. Ledger append-only, exportable; retain per schedule (basis proof after withdrawal for limitation periods). Withdrawal: one-click, no login walls where avoidable, propagate all systems + vendors under 24h, confirm to subject, test timed quarterly.

Refresh: re-consent on new purposes, material notice changes, or stale grants (2-year default review); legacy unprovable grants sunset via campaign. Children: age-gate + parental where regime requires; higher bar always.

Audit: yearly chain audits (grant -> use -> withdrawal -> deletion) with evidence; per-release dark-pattern scans; regulator-ready export pack. Consent receipt to subject on request.
