# Transfer Governance

Mechanisms (GDPR-shaped): adequacy decision (simplest, watch validity); SCCs + TIA + safeguards (standard); BCRs (groups, slow to approve); derogations (explicit consent, contract-necessary — narrow, documented). PIPL: security assessment/certification/SCC-CN per volume/sensitivity. Pick per route with counsel.

TIAs: destination law + practice (access requests, redress), data sensitivity + volume, safeguards effectiveness, residual risk conclusion. Route-specific facts; honest about surveillance risk; re-do on legal change. Copy-paste TIAs are findings waiting to happen.

Safeguards: encrypt in transit + at rest (keys home-held), pseudonymize/minimize before transfer, gate access (need-to-know, logging), contractual audit rights, challenge-request policy. Technical beats contractual alone.

Ops: register (route, mechanism, TIA date, review date); triggers (rulings, vendor subprocessors, scope change); yearly review; suspension runbook drilled (stop flow, localize, notify). Shadow-transfer discovery via SSO/spend/DNS sweeps.
