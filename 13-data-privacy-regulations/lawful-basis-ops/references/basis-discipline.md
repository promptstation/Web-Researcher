# Basis Discipline

Bases (GDPR-shaped, adapt per regime): consent (freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous + withdrawable); contract (necessary to perform); legal obligation; legitimate interests (purpose, necessity, balancing + safeguards + opt-out). Pick before collecting; document why others do not fit.

Purposes: narrow and explicit ('fraud screening of marketplace sellers') not catch-alls ('improve services'); one record per purpose; consumer wording matches internal; retention tied per purpose. New purpose = compatibility test -> re-basis or refuse.

LIAs: legitimate aim stated; necessity (no milder means); balancing (rights impact vs interest, safeguards, expectations, vulnerability of subjects); opt-out effective; review date. Sensitive data and children raise the bar sharply — counsel always.

Gates: launch requires basis pack; repurpose requires re-basis memo; yearly review or on flow change; creep log reviewed quarterly. Notices must mirror internal purposes — mismatch is a finding.
