# Course 15 — Ethical Scraping
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 15: Ethical Scraping
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Ethics Foundations for Scrapers**

**Summary:** How stakeholder mapping, harm analysis, and consent thinking ground every scraping decision beyond mere legality, with practical review habits.

**Absorbed Skill:** Ground scraping decisions in stakeholder, harm, and consent analysis.

---

**Politeness Engineering**

**Summary:** How impact budgets, adaptive rates, off-peak scheduling, caching, and footprint reporting make crawlers good citizens of the shared web.

**Absorbed Skill:** Crawl as a good citizen with budgets, adaptive rates, and footprint reports.

---

**Social License Operations**

**Summary:** How social license — acceptance beyond law — is earned with transparency, reciprocity, opt-outs, and community norms, and lost through extraction without return.

**Absorbed Skill:** Earn social license with transparency, reciprocity, and honored opt-outs.

---

**Personal Data Ethics Beyond Records**

**Summary:** How aggregation, mosaic effects, context violation, and power asymmetries harm even with lawful data — and how minimization, aggregation, and refusal prevent it.

**Absorbed Skill:** Prevent mosaic and context harms with minimization, aggregation, and refusal.

---

**Research Ethics Operations**

**Summary:** How IRB-style review, deception rules, debriefing, vulnerable-population protections, and pre-registration keep web research ethical and credible.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run web research with IRB-style review, no deception, and pre-registration.

---

**Competitive and Market Ethics**

**Summary:** How fair-play norms govern price/intel scraping: no sabotage, no theft of advantage, respect for makers, with build-vs-license discipline.

**Absorbed Skill:** Compete fairly with licensed-first data and no-sabotage norms.

---

**AI Training Data Ethics**

**Summary:** How consent, opt-outs (robots, ai.txt, registries), attribution, and creator compensation shape ethical AI datasets beyond legal minimums.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build AI datasets with honored opt-outs, provenance, and creator respect.

---

**Transparency and Accountability**

**Summary:** How methods publishing, limitation statements, correction policies, and external scrutiny make scraping operations trustworthy and improvable.

**Absorbed Skill:** Publish methods, limits, and corrections — inviting scrutiny that improves work.

---

**Incident Ethics: Stop, Disclose, Remediate**

**Summary:** How stop criteria, harm assessment, disclosure duties, and remediation with amends handle scraping incidents ethically — from over-collection to exposure.

**Absorbed Skill:** Handle scraping incidents with fast stops, honest disclosure, and amends.

---

**Culture and Governance**

**Summary:** How charters, review boards, training, incentives, and leadership modeling build organizations that choose right under pressure — the capstone habit.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build ethical culture with charters, boards, training, and aligned incentives.
