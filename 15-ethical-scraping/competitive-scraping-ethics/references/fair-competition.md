# Fair Competition

Hierarchy: licensed feed/API > polite scrape of open data > narrow excerpt with attribution > skip. Build-vs-buy memo per contested source; budget for data like infrastructure. Scraping a rival who sells the same feed is free-riding — negotiate first, accept price or scope, or skip.

Fair play: respect their terms and rates (rivalry does not suspend politeness); attribute where value derives; compensate (fees, traffic, data-back) proportionally; never misrepresent identity to gain advantage. Compete on product, not on extraction tricks.

Absolute bans (firing offenses): sabotage (fake orders, load attacks, review gaming for/against), deception (impersonation, false pretenses), theft (creds, insider data, hacked leaks), retaliation hacks. Refuse in writing; leadership backs publicly; protect refusers.

Exits: asked (C&D/ToS) -> pause + counsel + negotiate-or-exit; dealt (licensed) -> migrate + delete scrape stores; outbid -> wind down dated. Log conflicts; brief leadership; publish fair-play charter. Markets work when makers survive.
