# Mosaic Context

Mosaic: lawful fragments (name + city + employer + photo + posts) combine into doxxing, profiling, and targeting. Test: give fragments to outsiders, attempt re-identification and sensitive inference; if they succeed, so will adversaries. Aggregate-only publication for sensitive; delays blunt real-time stalking; fuzzing (bands, jitter, k-floors) defeats precision abuse.

Context (Nissenbaum-ish): data shared in one context (support group, dating, play) reused in another (employment, insurance, policing) violates integrity even when 'public'. Map origin->use->downstream; consent gap named; dignity cost weighed. Vulnerable subjects (minors, patients, dissidents, victims) = highest bar, often no-go.

Power: ask who gains leverage over whom (landlord/applicant, insurer/insured, state/dissident, stalker/target). Builds concentrating power over the vulnerable need extraordinary justification — usually refusal. Abuse-model every people-data feature: stalker, bigot, scammer, state.

Refusals: written memos (harm, analysis, alt offered) filed and protected; retaliation-free guarantee; leadership backs in writing. Publish refusal categories (people-search, stalking, discrimination, persecution tooling) as policy. Refusal is a deliverable.
