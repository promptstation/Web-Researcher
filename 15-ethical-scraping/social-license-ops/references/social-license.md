# Social License

License = ongoing acceptance by affected communities, beyond legal permission. Earned by: asking first (mods, admins, representatives), transparency (who/what/why/published), reciprocity (value sized to take: attribution, traffic, data-back, funding, tools), opt-outs that work (easy, fast, total incl. derivatives), and grievance paths with amends.

Read norms: robots + ToS + culture (rules, history, past scraper conflicts); lurk and learn before collecting; introduce yourself and intent; accept no gracefully. Sensitive communities (health, support, marginalized) get higher bars — often aggregate-only or no-go.

Opt-outs: discoverable (page + contact + robots-aware), one action, honored under 72h across raw/derived/backups, confirmed to requester, logged. Audit quarterly with test requests. Dark retention (keeping 'anonymized' copies against opt-out spirit) destroys license.

Repair: pause, listen, apologize specifically, delete per asks, make amends (donation, data return, policy change), re-earn slowly with new terms. Track license signals (complaints, blocks, sentiment) quarterly like revenue.
