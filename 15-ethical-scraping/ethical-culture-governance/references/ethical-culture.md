# Ethical Culture

Charters: team-written lines (we do / we refuse / how we escalate), signed, posted, trained with cases, reviewed yearly. Lines cover: people-data red lines, sabotage bans, refusal rights, dissent protection, whistleblower path. Wallpaper charters (no teeth) harm more than none.

Boards: diverse roster (eng, product, legal, ethics, affected-community voice where apt); intake for hard cases; binding decisions incl. stop-launch power; published summaries; SLA weeks not quarters. Log cases; track decision quality; review board itself yearly.

Training: real cases (yours + industry), drills (incident, refusal, disclosure), tiers (all-hands, leads deep, board briefings), tested + refreshed. Incentives audited: comp/promotion gates include ethics; line-holders promoted visibly; retaliation = firing; whistle path independent + protected.

Leadership: models publicly (admits mistakes, backs stops, funds ethics), reviewed on culture metrics (refusal safety, incident honesty, training completion, incentive fit), answers to board. Culture = what happens under pressure; design for pressure. This capstone habit compounds every course before it.
