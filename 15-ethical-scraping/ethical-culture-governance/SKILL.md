# Ethical Culture Governance

Make integrity the easy choice. You charter lines plainly, board hard cases with teeth, train with real cases and drills, promote line-holders, protect dissent and whistleblowing absolutely — with leaders modeling and answering.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- lived charters
- toothed boards
- trained teams
- aligned incentives
- answerable leaders

Ethics without power is decoration; boards can stop launches, and do.

## Use Cases

### Charter live

Trigger: user says 'set team values' or 'ethics charter'

Steps:

1. Draft with team
2. Sign publicly
3. Train cases
4. Review yearly

Result: Lived lines.

### Hard case

Trigger: user says 'consult the board' or 'tough call'

Steps:

1. Brief board
2. Deliberate
3. Decide binding
4. Publish apt

Result: Wise decision.

### Culture audit

Trigger: user says 'how ethical are we' or 'survey team'

Steps:

1. Measure signals
2. Fix incentives
3. Report + act
4. Re-measure

Result: Healthier culture.

Ask yourself before starting:

- What is the smallest verifiable outcome the user needs?
- What evidence will prove the result is correct?
- Which failures are most likely, and how will each be detected?
- What scope, budget, or policy constraints apply?
- What artifact must remain for audit or reuse?

## Core Output Requirements

Every delivery with this skill must include:

- Charter doc
- Board roster
- Case log
- Training plan
- Incentive audit
- Whistleblower path
- Culture metrics
- Leadership reviews

Never punish line-holding or run ethics as PR without power.

## Phase 1 — Charter Together

Draft with `scripts/charter_gen.py --team NAME`.

Lines + refusals + escalation.

Signed + posted + trained.

## Phase 2 — Board with Teeth

Diverse roster; can stop launches.

Cases logged; decisions binding.

Publish apt summaries.

## Phase 3 — Train Real

Cases from your incidents.

Drills quarterly.

Test + refresh yearly.

## Phase 4 — Align and Model

Promote holders; audit incentives.

Whistle path protected.

Leaders reviewed on ethics.

## Troubleshooting

### Charter wallpaper

Cause: No teeth or training

Fix:

1. Board power real
2. Cases trained
3. Violations consequenced
4. Re-launch

### Fear culture

Cause: Retaliation history

Fix:

1. Protect visibly
2. Anonymous paths
3. Independent review
4. Rebuild slowly

### Ethics vs bonus

Cause: Misaligned incentives

Fix:

1. Audit comp
2. Add ethics gates
3. Reward holds
4. Fix or lose trust

## Decision Heuristic

Before adding complexity, ask:

1. What outcome requires this step?
2. What evidence justifies it?
3. What happens when it fails?
4. What is the cheaper alternative?
5. What proves quality did not regress?
6. What must be logged for audit?
7. What is the rollback plan?

If the main justification is "more", prove the outcome needs it first.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid wallpaper charters; toothless boards; theater training; misaligned bonuses; retaliation cultures; unreviewed leaders.
