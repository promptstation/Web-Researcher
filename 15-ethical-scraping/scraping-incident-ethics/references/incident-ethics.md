# Incident Ethics

Criteria (stop when): PII/sensitive spill detected, ToS/authorization breach suspected, credible harm report, target distress/block wave, team member raises red flag. Authority: any operator can halt their scope; leads can halt all; kill-switches tested quarterly; halt-then-discuss culture.

Triage (hours): what happened, scope (records/systems/time), who affected + how badly, still spreading?, evidence preserved. Counsel + ethics looped; memo in 24h. Preserve-then-purge carefully: evidence for review, then delete per duties.

Disclosure: affected parties notified duly (what, impact, remedies); public notice where public data/harm; regulators per duty; users of derived data warned. Templates pre-drafted; plain language; no minimizing. Silence compounds harm and liability.

Remediation+: delete/retract, fix root, compensate/amends (credit, donation, free service, policy change sized to harm), blameless review into prevention tasks owned+dated, trust metrics tracked (complaints, sentiment, retention). Drill scenarios yearly.
