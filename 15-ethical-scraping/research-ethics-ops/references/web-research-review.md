# Web Research Review

Review (Belmont/Menlo: respect/beneficence/justice): protocol states questions, methods, subject risks, consent, data handling, debrief; board (internal + external where apt) approves pre-field; tiered (exempt/expedited/full) keeps speed with rigor. Log all decisions.

Deception: fake accounts, sockpuppets, false pretenses, undisclosed observation of sensitive spaces — presumptively barred; extraordinary exception needs full board + no alternative + debrief + minimal harm. Design honestly first: consented panels, public data with care, simulations, partnerships.

Vulnerable: minors, patients, dissidents, victims, economically precarious — screen high; exclude or robustly safeguard (consent, minimization, aggregation, security); extra review. Never trade their safety for findings.

Credibility: pre-register questions/methods/analyses; publish nulls; repro packages (code+data+env); limit statements; welcome corrections. Findings that cannot reproduce or methods that cannot bear light are not findings.
