# Risk Patterns

Pattern guide (informational, not advice): bypassing logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, or IP blocks raises access-law risk; violating explicit automation bans risks account and contract action; scraping personal data triggers privacy regimes; republishing creative content triggers copyright review. Public, unauthenticated, unbanished pages are lowest risk but never zero.

Tests: could a reasonable operator call this unauthorized? Is there a technical barrier being worked around? Would the plan survive a terms quote in daylight? Any yes-or-maybe means amber minimum and counsel before proceeding.

Briefing: one page with facts, sources, terms quotes, technical posture, alternatives considered, and the exact question. Stop-work triggers: counsel says pause, operator objects, new barrier appears, or scope creeps.

Logs: date, decider, sources, grades, terms quotes, counsel notes, decision, review date. Immutable, retained per policy. This skill never substitutes for qualified legal counsel.
