# Course 04 — Bot Detection Evasion
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 4: Bot Detection Evasion
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Bot Management Landscape**

**Summary:** How Cloudflare, DataDome, PerimeterX, Akamai, and Kasada structure detection (signals, scores, actions), what challenge and block pages look like, and how to classify outcomes to respond lawfully instead of guessing.

**Absorbed Skill:** Explain bot-management pipelines and classify challenge, block, and rate outcomes from page evidence.

---

**Detection Signals Taxonomy**

**Summary:** Which signals feed bot scores across network, TLS, HTTP, browser, and behavior layers, how to inventory what your own clients emit, and how to keep legitimate automation transparent and consistent.

**Absorbed Skill:** Inventory client-emitted signals per layer and keep legitimate automation transparent, consistent, and documented.

---

**Behavioral Analysis and Pacing**

**Summary:** How request timing, path order, session depth, and diurnal patterns reveal automation, how to measure your own behavior footprint, and how to pace collection at respectful human-scale rhythms.

**Absorbed Skill:** Measure automation behavior footprints and pace collection at respectful, sustainable rhythms.

---

**Legitimate Automation Identity**

**Summary:** How to identify crawlers honestly via user agents, robots.txt compliance, contact pages, and terms review, so operators can understand, meter, and allowlist your automation.

**Absorbed Skill:** Establish honest crawler identity with compliant user agents, robots adherence, and documented purpose.

---

**Polite Crawling and Rate Discipline**

**Summary:** How to budget request rates per host, honor Retry-After and crawl-delay, adapt to 429s with backoff, and run adaptive throttles that protect both targets and your own access.

**Absorbed Skill:** Budget per-host rates, honor server pacing signals, and adapt throttles automatically to stay welcome.

---

**Access Negotiation: APIs and Permission**

**Summary:** How to find official data channels, write access requests that get approved, negotiate rate and scope, and maintain access relationships with renewals and compliance reports.

**Absorbed Skill:** Discover official channels, write winning access requests, and maintain access with renewals and reports.

---

**Challenge Response Operations**

**Summary:** How to detect challenges and blocks in pipelines, back off automatically, park affected jobs, queue human review where permitted, and resume safely with canaries instead of retry storms.

**Absorbed Skill:** Detect challenges in pipelines and execute backoff, park, review, and canary-resume without storms.

---

**Testing Your Own Defenses**

**Summary:** How to red-team bot defenses on systems you own or are hired to test, with scoped rules of engagement, safe probes, finding reports, and fix verification.

**Absorbed Skill:** Scope and run defensive self-tests on owned systems and verify fixes with retests.

---

**Legal Boundaries Around Evasion**

**Summary:** How to think about CFAA-style access law, terms enforcement, trespass, and copyright limits on scraping, when to escalate to counsel, and how to keep decision logs that show restraint (informational, not legal advice).

**Absorbed Skill:** Spot legal-risk patterns in scraping plans, escalate to counsel early, and keep restraint-showing decision logs.

---

**Block Monitoring and Incident Response**

**Summary:** How to monitor block and challenge rates fleet-wide, alert on wave signatures, run incident response with roles and timelines, and close with postmortems that prevent recurrence.

**Absorbed Skill:** Monitor block signals fleet-wide and run incident response from detection to postmortem.
