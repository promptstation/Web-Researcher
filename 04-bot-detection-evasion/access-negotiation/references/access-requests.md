# Access Requests

Discovery: developer portals, /developers, /api, data programs, bulk/download pages, sitemap feeds, RSS/Atom, public datasets, and partner programs. Ask sales or support for the data owner when hidden. Prefer bulk over crawl, feeds over pages.

Request template: who you are; what data exactly; why (purpose); how much and how often; retention and security; what you give back (attribution, findings, traffic). Propose 30-day review. Keep first asks small; grow on trust.

Stewardship: 20 percent quota headroom; vaulted keys with rotation dates; per-grant usage logs; renewal ticklers 30 days out. Report usage and incidents before asked.

Denials: thank, ask what would qualify, narrow and re-ask once, accept final no. Denied scope is closed scope; route around nothing.
