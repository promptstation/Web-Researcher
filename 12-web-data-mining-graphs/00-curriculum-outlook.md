# Course 12 — Web Data Mining & Graph Theory
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 12: Web Data Mining & Graph Theory
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Crawl Frontiers and Scheduling**

**Summary:** How priority frontiers with politeness, per-host queues, seen-sets, and revisit scheduling crawl domains completely without hammering anyone.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run polite priority frontiers with per-host queues and revisit scheduling.

---

**Link Graphs and Ranking**

**Summary:** How to build link graphs from crawls, compute PageRank and HITS honestly, handle sinks and spam, and use rank for crawl priority and quality.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build link graphs and compute rank honestly for priority and quality.

---

**Communities and Clustering**

**Summary:** How label propagation, Louvain-style modularity, and topical clustering find communities in web graphs for discovery, spam detection, and navigation.

**Absorbed Skill:** Find graph communities for discovery, spam detection, and navigation.

---

**Content Mining: Terms, Topics and Trends**

**Summary:** How TF-IDF, keyphrase extraction, topic sketching, and trend detection turn crawled text into terms, topics, and movements you can act on.

**Absorbed Skill:** Mine crawled text for terms, topics, and trends with eval'd methods.

---

**Structured Discovery: Sitemaps and Feeds**

**Summary:** How sitemaps, RSS/Atom, robots, and well-known endpoints structure discovery, with parsing, change signals, and coverage reconciliation.

**Absorbed Skill:** Drive discovery from sitemaps and feeds with change signals and coverage proof.

---

**Change Mining and Freshness Analytics**

**Summary:** How fetch histories, hash timelines, and change-rate models reveal update patterns that drive revisit scheduling and freshness SLAs.

**Absorbed Skill:** Mine change patterns from histories to drive revisits and freshness SLAs.

---

**Quality and Spam Mining**

**Summary:** How content, link, and behavior signals combine into quality scores that filter spam, thin content, and junk from corpora and indexes.

**Absorbed Skill:** Score web quality with combined signals and filter junk provably.

---

**Web Knowledge Graphs**

**Summary:** How entity-relation triples extracted from crawls build queryable knowledge graphs with provenance, confidence, and conflict handling.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build web knowledge graphs with provenanced triples and conflict handling.

---

**Graph Queries and Traversal**

**Summary:** How BFS/DFS, shortest paths, neighborhood expansion, and pattern matching answer questions over web graphs at interactive speed.

**Absorbed Skill:** Answer questions over graphs with traversals, paths, and patterns.

---

**Mining Ethics and Measurement**

**Summary:** How representativeness audits, bias measurement, consent boundaries, and transparent methodology keep web mining honest and defensible.

**Absorbed Skill:** Keep mining honest with representativeness audits, bias checks, and open methodology.
