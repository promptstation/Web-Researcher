# Course 14 — Computer Crime Laws
## Curriculum Content Outlook (Researching-Outline workflow, compressed to ≤15 sections)

Source course list: `Web researcher and scrapper.txt` — Course 14: Computer Crime Laws
Method: Researching-Outline.md workflow (Steps 1–8) + beginner→expert progression research + compression into groups.
Sections: 10 (within the ≤15 limit). Each section = Summary (what it teaches) + Absorbed Skill (capability gained).

---

**Unauthorized Access Literacy**

**Summary:** How CFAA-style unauthorized-access law frames permission, how Van Buren narrowed exceeds-authorized-access, and how to evidence authorization for every system you touch.

**Absorbed Skill:** Evidence authorization for every system with scope records and expiry.

---

**Copyright and Database Rights**

**Summary:** How copyright, fair use/fair dealing, database rights, and takedown processes constrain collecting and reusing web content lawfully.

**Absorbed Skill:** Collect and reuse content within copyright and database rights, takedown-ready.

---

**Contracts: Terms and Acceptable Use**

**Summary:** How browsewrap vs clickwrap, ToS change monitoring, and contract-vs-law boundaries govern scraping relationships, with review runbooks and negotiation paths.

**Absorbed Skill:** Govern target relationships with tracked terms, change alerts, and negotiation.

---

**Anti-circumvention Boundaries**

**Summary:** How DMCA 1201-style anti-circumvention law treats TPMs, what counts as circumvention vs interoperable access, and how decision trees keep you on the lawful side.

**Absorbed Skill:** Navigate TPMs with circumvention decision trees and counsel gates.

---

**Fraud, Identity and Credential Integrity**

**Summary:** How fraud, identity-theft, and credential-misuse laws draw hard lines around accounts, and how 1:1 account discipline, no-fake identities, and credential hygiene keep operations clean.

**Absorbed Skill:** Keep account operations clean with 1:1 discipline, real identities, and hygiene.

---

**Evidence Preservation and Chain of Custody**

**Summary:** How write-once collection, hashing, timestamping, custody logs, and legal-hold alignment preserve digital evidence admissibly.

**Absorbed Skill:** Preserve digital evidence with hashing, custody logs, and hold alignment.

---

**Disclosure and Law Enforcement Liaison**

**Summary:** How vulnerability disclosure, data-request response (subpoenas, warrants, preservation letters), and LE liaison run through counsel with logged, minimal, lawful cooperation.

**Absorbed Skill:** Handle disclosures and data requests through counsel with minimal lawful response.

---

**International Cybercrime Coordination**

**Summary:** How the Budapest Convention, MLATs, data-localization, and conflicting-compulsion problems shape cross-border scraping, storage, and incident response.

**Absorbed Skill:** Navigate cross-border cybercrime law with jurisdiction maps and MLAT paths.

---

**Trade Secrets and Confidential Data**

**Summary:** How trade-secret law protects reasonably-guarded secrets, how to avoid acquiring or spreading others' secrets in scraping, and how to guard your own.

**Absorbed Skill:** Avoid others' secrets and guard your own with reasonable measures.

---

**Cyber-law Records Program**

**Summary:** How risk registers, control frameworks, training, audits, and board reporting compose an organizational cyber-law Records program that prevents, detects, and corrects.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run an organizational cyber-law Records program with controls and audits.
