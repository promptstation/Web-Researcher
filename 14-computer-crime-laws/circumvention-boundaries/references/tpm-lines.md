# TPM Lines

TPMs: access controls (passwords, keys, gates), copy controls (DRM, encryption), and measures controlling use. Circumvention = descramble/decrypt/avoid/bypass/impair without authority. Ordinary interoperable use (standard clients, documented APIs, user-directed browsers) differs from defeating measures — but the line is fact-specific and regime-specific; counsel draws it.

Exemptions (US-shaped, narrow): security testing with authorization, encryption research factors, interoperability to achieve program-to-program compatibility, accessibility. Each has multi-factor tests + scope limits + often notice/record duties. Claim narrowly with evidence; self-serving reads fail.

Decision tree: 1) Is there a gate? 2) Do we have authority to pass it? 3) Is our method ordinary use or defeat? 4) Does a specific exemption fit with all factors? 5) Counsel written call? No/unsure anywhere = stop and reroute. Log every walk.

Team shields: written refusal path for unlawful asks; alt-path brainstorming norm; training yearly; no retaliation for holding lines. Prefer APIs, partnerships, and public sources — creativity belongs in lawful paths.
