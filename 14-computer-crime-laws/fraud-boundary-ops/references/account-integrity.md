# Account Integrity

Lines: creating fake identities, using others' credentials, trafficking access, and impersonation cross fraud/identity-theft statutes in most regimes — plus platform bans and contract breach. Testing needs test accounts from the platform, never fakes. No deadline justifies crossing; refuse in writing and offer lawful paths.

Discipline: every account maps to one human + one purpose + defined scope; shared/service accounts named, owned, vaulted, and logged per use; credentials unique per account, vaulted, rotated 90d or on trigger; MFA everywhere offered (hardware/phishing-resistant for critical). Sharing = finding, instantly remediated.

Audits: quarterly 1:1 attestation; vault coverage scans; rotation compliance; vendor account review (demand named seats in contracts). Compromise: revoke -> rotate -> scope (logs: what touched) -> notify (platform, affected, legal) -> postmortem -> harden. Drill yearly.

Records: inventory, map, vault report, rotation log, attestations, drill records. Account integrity is cultural: praise refusals, never punish line-holding.
