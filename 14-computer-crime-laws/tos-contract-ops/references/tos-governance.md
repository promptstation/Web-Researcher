# ToS Governance

Reading: find scraping/automation, API, rate-limit, IP/license, termination, and governing-law clauses; note browsewrap (posted terms, weaker notice) vs clickwrap (affirmative assent, stronger); enforceability varies by regime and notice quality — counsel calls gray. Risk-tier: green (API offered), yellow (silent/ambiguous), red (explicit ban + enforcement history).

Tracking: register per target (URL, version hash, snapshot date, owner); re-fetch weekly-ish; diff and alert on change; assess impact in days. Positions: comply (operate inside terms), negotiate (API/deal), avoid (no-go with reasons) — memo'd, counsel-signed on gray, review-dated.

Negotiation: lead with value (attribution, traffic, fees), ask API/data tier, paper SLAs + IP + termination; migrate off scraping when dealt. Hostility (C&D, blocks): pause first, counsel responds, log everything, negotiate or exit — never escalate technically.

Hygiene: no fake acceptance, no ToS-evasion trickery; snapshots retained as evidence; quarterly relationship reviews. Terms are relationships to manage, not puzzles to defeat.
