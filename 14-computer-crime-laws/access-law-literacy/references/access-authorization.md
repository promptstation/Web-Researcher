# Access Authorization

Shape (US CFAA as archetype, adapt locally): liability for accessing without authorization or exceeding it; Van Buren narrowed exceeds-authorized to gate-upheld restrictions (areas off-limits), not purpose misuse — but contracts, state laws, and foreign regimes still bite. Operational rule: written permission naming system, actions, data, time, purpose — before touching anything.

Scope anatomy: systems (hosts/APIs/accounts), permitted actions (read/test/collect), data classes, time window, purpose, exclusions (no-go areas), contacts, revocation path. Signed by someone who can grant; filed immutable; machine-readable copy for tooling gates.

Hygiene: expiry on every grant (30-365d by risk); re-verify to renew; revoke on role/project end; quarterly access reviews; tooling checks scope before runs. Gray (public-but-gated, ToS-vs-law, research exemptions): counsel memo before access, never creativity.

Proof packs: grant + identity + logs (who/when/what) + scope-match statement; challenge response within days; audit trail append-only. Train teams yearly; test with tabletop challenges. Literacy enables questions; counsel gives answers.
