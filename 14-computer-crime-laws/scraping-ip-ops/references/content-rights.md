# Content Rights

Copyright: original expression (articles, photos, designs) protected; facts/ideas not; scraping copies implicate reproduction + adaptation. Fair use (US, 4 factors: purpose/character incl. transformativeness, nature, amount/substantiality, market effect) is case-by-case — commercial whole-work copying rarely fair; research/transformative/minimal stronger. Fair dealing (UK/EU variants) narrower, enumerated purposes.

Database rights (EU sui generis): substantial extraction/reutilization of database contents needs maker permission even absent copyright; repeated small takes can aggregate. Terms + robots + TPMs add contract/access layers. Always triage: license > exception > limit > skip.

Provenance: per item (source URL, fetched_at, license/rights basis, ToS snapshot); propagate to chunks/derivatives; takedown maps item->source instantly. Reuse scoping: internal research < published analysis < commercial redistribution in risk; re-assess per reuse tier.

Takedowns: validate (work, location, authority, good-faith), remove 24-72h, notify supplier/user, offer counter-notice, log all, repeat-infringer policy enforced. Counsel on gray fair-use and database calls. Not legal advice.
